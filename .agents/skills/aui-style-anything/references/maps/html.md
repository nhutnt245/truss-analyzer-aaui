# Map: plain HTML elements to levels and recipes

For `references/restyle.md`, step 3. The recipe ids are in `../atomicaui/references/standard/recipes/` (the page is named in each row). Copy a recipe whole; change texts, ids, names and values only.

| Old element | Level | Recipe (page) | Watch |
|---|---|---|---|
| `<button>`, `<input type="submit">` | 2 | `button-main` (the one main action of a bar or dialog), `button-default`, `button-quiet`, `button-disabled` (button) | Keep `type` and the handler. Disabled: swap the whole class list and set `disabled` and `data-disabled`. A red or green button has no look of its own: the kit picks main or default |
| `<button>` with an icon only | 2 | `icon-button` (button) | `aria-label` is required (AUI-11) |
| `<a href>` | 3 | The style-anything piece "one thing to click" (`SKILL.md`, kind B), root `data-aui-styled="<name>"`, class list from `node scripts/aui-class.mjs` | Keep `href`. `<a>` with a click handler and no `href` is a button: level 2. A link of a top bar's own links (in the bar or its `<nav>`, not in a breadcrumb, pagination, menu or tabs there) is not a piece: the nav bar row below (`top-bar-links`, level 2) |
| `<input type="text">`, `email`, `search` | 2 | `field-input`, `field-input-readonly` (field) | The label sits above the control. Keep `id`, `name`, `placeholder`, `required` |
| `<textarea>` | 2 | `field-textarea` (form) | Keep `rows` out: the box grows with its text |
| `<input type="number">` with a unit | 1 | `field-number-unit` (form) | The unit text sits inside the field |
| `<input type="date">` | 2 | `field-date` (field) | The browser draws the calendar; the value stays `yyyy-mm-dd` |
| `<select>` | 1 | `select-open` written closed (field), with `aui/aui-behaviour.js` | The hidden input carries the select's old `id`, its `name` and the value. An option whose value is not its text names it in `data-value` (`data-value=""` for an empty one): `aui/aui-behaviour.js` writes the picked option's `data-value` into the hidden input, and `check-behaviour` reads it there. A value kept another way (a script of your own that maps the text back) is a difference, and `restyle-done` says NOT DONE on it: never "fixed" by editing the check |
| `<input type="checkbox">`, `radio`, a switch | 1 | `checkbox` (field), `field-radio`, `field-switch` (form) | Add the toggle the recipe's "You add" line says. Keep `name` and `value` on a hidden input |
| `<label>` and its control | 2 | Inside the field recipe | `for` and `id` stay a pair |
| A form's rows of fields | 2 | `view-stack`, `view-row` (view); `field-error` for a failed field (form) | Keep `<form>` and its submit. Show the error text only while invalid |
| A tree (`role="tree"`, nested `ul`, folder rows) | 1 | `tree` (tree) | Rows and the fold arrow are paint text on the surface: no border, no tonal box, no button look; only the picked row is filled, its `data-aui-tree-view-row` taking `aui-color-solid-surface-alt` (`../atomicaui/references/standard/recipes/tree.md`). Keep its roles, `aria-expanded`, item identifiers (`data-key` or `id` values, as they were) and folding script; a restyle adds no `data-key` the old tree lacked. TreeView indents each level. A tree that never folds is the same markup with every group open and each `aui-tree-view-chevron` empty, and no folding script. Never a pane or a tree of buttons |
| `<table>`, `thead`, `tbody` | 2 | `table-display`, or `table-scroll` when its columns do not fit 390 px; a row to pick `table-row-selected`; a status column `chip-status` (table, chip) only where its cells were already badges or chips: a status in plain text stays text | Keep your row markup: the recipe's cells are `td` with the recipe's classes. No `width` attribute |
| Tabs built with `div`s | 1 | `tabs`, `tabs-closable` (tabs), with `aui/aui-behaviour.js` | Every tab names its panel with `aria-controls` |
| A modal `div` | 1 | `dialog` closed inside `<div data-aui-dialog-layers hidden>` (dialog) | The opener has `data-aui-dialog-open`; footer buttons close it. Delete your show and hide handlers and record the difference. A footer button that posts a form leaves the page, and `check-behaviour` cannot follow it: close the dialog, then act |
| A message box (`.alert`, `role="alert"`) | 1 | `message-banner` (view); with an icon, `message-banner-with-icon` (view) | One line of text; the colour never says it alone. An icon in it is the kit Font Awesome icon of `message-banner-with-icon`: its whole icon slot (the `aui-banner-icon` span with its `<i>`), only the `fa-` name of the `<i>` changed; never a copied `<svg>` or `<img>`, and never the `<i>` without its span (it would sit on a row of its own above the text) |
| A status text line (`role="status"` with no icon, heading, badge or control inside, a line a script fills) | 2 | Plain text in its existing element: the span classes of any view recipe (view) | Keep its role, script and text. Never turn it into a banner, chip or tone. Anything else inside (a paragraph, `strong`, `sup`, a visually hidden span, a plain wrapper) keeps it a text line; only an icon (also an `<svg>` or `<img>`), a progress bar, a heading, a badge, a button or another control inside makes a `role="status"` box a message box. That message box is restyled by the row above. An icon in it is the kit Font Awesome icon of `message-banner-with-icon` (view): its whole icon slot (the `aui-banner-icon` span with its `<i>`), only the `fa-` name of the `<i>` changed; never a copied `<svg>` or `<img>` |
| A card, a panel | 2 | `view-panel` (view), `section-card` (cards) | Not a pane: a pane is `../atomicaui/references/standard/recipes/layout.md` |
| A header, a footer, a nav bar | 2 | `view-toolbar` (view) inside the page frame; `top-bar-links` (top-bar) for links | The bar keeps its element (the top-bar blocks are for a new page, on a `<div>`): a bar of buttons and links, each link with the whole link class list of `top-bar-links`. Add ``- Styled piece `<name>`: what was missing, which screen, which file`` to `GAPS.md` only for a piece that needs styling beyond the recipes. A link in a bar, a `<nav>`, a list of tabs or a menu, a link that marks the current page (`aria-current`) and a tab have no underline: give each the class `aui-text-decoration-none`, keep the focus ring and `aria-current` (the check reads those places and a labelled group of two or more links in a row; a link inside a paragraph keeps its underline) |
| A heading, a paragraph | 2 | `type-heading` (view); plain text takes the span classes of any view recipe | `aui-margin-0`: the gap of the box around makes the space |
| `<img>`, `<canvas>`, `<svg>` chart, `<iframe>` | 4 | `references/align.md` | The picture and the frame keep their own pixels |
| Anything with a popup, arrow keys or drag | gap | None | Restyled where it is: its markup, roles and behaviour stay, its look comes from the tokens and the nearest part (`SKILL.md`); for a styled piece add ``- Styled piece `<name>`: what was missing, which screen, which file`` to `GAPS.md`; what cannot be styled is one line in `GAPS.md` |

## Page frame
A page of panes: the `view-pane` recipe (layout). Below 768 px the panes stack by themselves when the page loads `aui/aui-behaviour.js`. A bar above it marks the frame `data-aui-panes="page under-bar"`; a page inside a layout you keep marks it `data-aui-panes="page in-layout"` (`references/restyle.md`, step 1). `html` and `body` fill the window (`../atomicaui/references/standard/recipes/layout.md`).

## Delete last
The old stylesheet (a shared one that a page outside the scope still uses stays (`references/restyle.md`)), `style` attributes, classes of the old look, spacing classes (`mt-3`, `p-2`); a class a kept widget script needs stays (`references/maps/bootstrap.md`). Space comes from the gap of the box around (AUI-05). Deleted means deleted from every page of the scope, and no new stylesheet of your own replaces it: `node ../atomicaui/scripts/restyle-done.mjs` (`references/restyle.md`, step 6) fails a page that still links one.
